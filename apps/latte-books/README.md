# Latte Books

Latte Books is fast, free and open-source desktop accounting for Windows, macOS and Linux, written from scratch in Rust and drawn with GPUI and GPUI Kit. Keep your books yourself, or let the AI assistant you already use keep them with you.

## Built for speed

Latte Books opens in under a second, uses about 18 MB of memory and is a 25 MB download. Each company is a single file you own, with no account and no cloud.

## Complete accounting

- **Sales and purchases:** quotes, invoices, payments, returns and journal entries, in any currency
- **Inventory:** items, stock, locations, shipments and purchase receipts
- **Point of sale,** with pricing rules, coupons and loyalty points
- **Reports:** General Ledger, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Stock Ledger and Stock Balance, to print or save as CSV
- **India GST:** GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, by month or by quarter for QRMP, with reverse charge and self-invoices
- **Charts of accounts** for 12 countries, and a standard one for the rest
- **Print to PDF** with templates you can edit
- **17 languages,** with community translations

## Built for AI

Latte Books ships an open-source MCP server, so Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT Codex or Cursor connect in a click. Ask your books questions, draft invoices or record a month of bills in plain words. The assistant works through the app, so it can only do what you could: new documents start as drafts, and it asks before posting anything.

## Switching from Frappe Books

Latte Books opens company files from Frappe Books v0.36.0 as they are.
