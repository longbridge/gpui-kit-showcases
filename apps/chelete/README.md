# Chelete

Chelete is a keyboard-driven personal finance tracker for Linux. It brings accounts, transactions and spending categories into a native desktop app with a local SQLite database.

## Understand your finances

The overview shows your total balance, income, expenses and recent transactions, alongside spending by category. Account views keep bank, savings, cash and credit card balances together, while the transaction list connects each entry to its account and category.

## Navigate from the keyboard

Keyboard shortcuts switch between views, and a command palette provides quick access to actions. Collapse the sidebar when you want more space for your data.

## Follow your desktop theme

Chelete integrates with Omarchy and updates its appearance when the desktop theme changes.
