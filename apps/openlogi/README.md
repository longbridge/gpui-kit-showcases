# OpenLogi

OpenLogi is an open-source, native alternative to Logitech Options+ for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Written in Rust, it configures supported Logitech devices directly through HID++ without an account or telemetry.

## Controls that fit your workflow

Select a button on the interactive device diagram and assign an action. Per-device bindings include navigation, custom shortcuts, and application launchers. Pointer controls provide adjustable DPI and presets, while SmartShift supports ratcheted scrolling, free spinning, or automatic switching based on scrolling speed.

## Connected devices at a glance

The device view shows connected hardware, battery levels, and charging status. OpenLogi communicates through Bolt, Unifying, and Lightspeed receivers, Bluetooth, or USB. Bolt pairing is available in the app; pairing support for Unifying and Lightspeed is still in development.

## Local settings

Bindings stay in a portable TOML file on your computer. Device illustrations may be fetched from a public CDN, and update checks are optional and disabled by default.

OpenLogi remains under active development. Per-application profiles and full Logitech Flow functionality are roadmap items. The project is independent of Logitech.
