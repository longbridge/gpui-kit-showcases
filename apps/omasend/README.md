# Omasend

Omasend is a desktop file-sharing app built for Omarchy. It copies, pastes, and sends files, folders, screenshots, recordings, and text to your other devices over the local network, with no account and no cloud upload.

## Send from the clipboard

Paste whatever is on the clipboard and it becomes a transfer: an image, a screenshot, a snippet of text, or a note. Files and folders can also be dragged into the window or chosen from the file picker, and everything queued for sending is previewed before it leaves the device.

## Nearby devices, discovered for you

Devices running Omasend or LocalSend on the same network appear in the Nearby list as they announce themselves. Pick one, send, and the receiving side decides whether to accept, so nothing arrives unannounced.

## Transfers stay on your network

Content travels directly between the two devices over an encrypted connection, never through a server. Received files land in Downloads and are kept alongside existing files instead of overwriting them.

## Watch the transfer

Progress and speed are visible while a transfer runs, a transfer can be cancelled part-way, and the current session keeps a history of what moved in each direction.

## At home on the desktop

Omasend follows the active Omarchy theme and retints with it, offers System, Light, and Dark appearances, and speaks English and 简体中文. Sending, pasting, and adding files each have a keyboard shortcut, and the app can stay resident so it is ready the moment something needs to move.

## Keep it current

Updates are checked from the menu and applied inside the app, and the restart waits until transfers in flight have finished.
